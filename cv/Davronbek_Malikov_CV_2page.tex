\section*{Summary}
Ph.D. in AI Convergence Engineering and AI Engineer \& Team Lead at TripleH, leading the team that builds on-premise
multi-agent LLM systems for enterprise clients. Fine-tunes open-weight LLMs with SFT (LoRA, QLoRA) and DPO on limited GPU

GraphRAG, LangGraph agents, and LangSmith and Arize Phoenix monitoring.

\smallskip

improving prediction accuracy by up to 17\%, deployed as live apps.
